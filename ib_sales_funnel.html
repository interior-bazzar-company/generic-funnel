Questions

Good to know.

Is each enquiry really exclusive to me?

Yes. Every enquiry is routed to one business only — never shared or resold to your competitors. That exclusivity is the heart of how we work, and the main thing that sets us apart from directories like Justdial or IndiaMART.

How do you verify an enquiry before sending it?

Each one passes a multi-level check — genuine interest, real intent and urgency — and is then confirmed by a direct call from our team. By the time it reaches you, it's a real person ready to talk.

How is this different from Justdial or IndiaMART?

They sell the same enquiry to many businesses at once, so you compete on price the moment you call. We do the opposite: one verified enquiry, one business — plus a profile and portfolio to build your brand, not just a stream of contacts.

What does it cost?

It's an annual subscription, priced to how deeply you want enquiries qualified. Founding members lock 25% off all Signature plans, with EMI available. We share the exact numbers on a short call once we understand your business — so you only pay for what fits, with no surprises and no obligation.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. We offer flexible payment, including EMI and installment options, so you can spread the cost rather than pay it all upfront. We'll walk you through the plan that fits your business on your call.

How soon will I start receiving enquiries?

Once your profile is set up and verified, enquiries are matched and routed as they come in. Volume depends on demand in your category and city, which we'll walk you through honestly on the call.
